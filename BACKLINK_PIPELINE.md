# Backlink Acquisition Pipeline

Ethical, zero-cost backlink strategy (§36–§37, §64–§65). No paid links, no PBNs, no automated blasts, no exact-match anchor-text campaigns.

## Target link sources (by relevance to a Damietta civil/commercial law firm)
- Egyptian legal publications and legal-news sites (commentary/guest contribution opportunities)
- Business and real-estate publications (the real-estate legal checklist, once written, is a natural pitch asset — see `CIVIL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md` gap #2)
- Damietta local news and business sites
- Professional associations (Bar Association, if it links member firms)
- Resource/guide pages on other legal or business sites that already link out to legal-education content

## Link-worthy assets to build first (digital PR, §77)
Content is the leverage — nobody links to a plain service page. Priority order, matching the content-gap priorities in `CIVIL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md`:
1. Real-estate buyer's legal due-diligence checklist (highest earned-link potential — practical, shareable, references-worthy)
2. Contract review checklist (drafting-clauses article, expanded into a standalone checklist format)
3. Inheritance/estate division guide (already has one article — could be expanded into the "digital PR asset" format once the disputes-among-heirs piece exists)

## Outreach anchor-text discipline (§65)
When pitching or when a link is earned, prefer:
- Brand: "مكتب آل حراز" / "Al Harraz Law Firm"
- Bare URL: alharrazlaw.com
- Natural descriptive phrase: e.g., "دليل قانوني" rather than an exact-match keyword like "محامي مدني دمياط" repeated across multiple placements

## Evaluation criteria before pursuing any specific link (§64)
Relevance to legal/business audience > raw traffic > editorial placement (earned within real content, not a sitewide footer link) > any third-party "authority score."
