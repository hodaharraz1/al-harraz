# Google Review Growth Playbook

Honest, zero-cost review acquisition. No fake reviews, no purchased reviews, no review gating (asking only happy clients to leave one while filtering out others is against Google's policy and this program's own rules — §34, §66).

## When to ask
- Right after a matter concludes successfully (case resolved, contract closed, consultation delivered value) — never mid-matter, and never in a way that could be read as conditioning service on a review.
- Never ask in exchange for a discount, favor, or any consideration — that's incentivized review activity, against policy.

## How to ask (message template — Arabic, WhatsApp/SMS-appropriate)

```
شكرًا لثقتكم في مكتب آل حراز للمحاماة والاستشارات القانونية.
لو تسمحوا، تقييمكم لتجربتكم معانا هيساعد عملاء تانيين يوصلولنا:
[GBP review link]
تقييمكم يهمنا سواء كان إيجابي أو في أي ملاحظة تحب تقولها.
```

Key elements: thank them, make the link one tap away, explicitly welcome any feedback (not just positive) — this is what keeps the process compliant with "no gating."

## Follow-up timing
- Send the request within 24–48 hours of matter conclusion, while the experience is fresh.
- If no response after 1 week, one polite follow-up is acceptable. Do not send more than two total requests for the same matter.

## Review response templates

**Positive review:**
```
شكرًا جزيلاً لتقييمكم الكريم. سعداء إننا قدرنا نساعدكم، ونتمنى نكون دايمًا عند حسن ظنكم.
مكتب آل حراز للمحاماة والاستشارات القانونية
```

**Negative/critical review:**
```
شكرًا لتواصلكم ولملاحظاتكم. نأسف لو تجربتكم معانا مكنتش زي ما كنتم متوقعين.
يسعدنا نتواصل معكم مباشرة لفهم الموضوع بالتفصيل ومحاولة حله — تقدروا تتواصلوا معنا على [phone].
```
Never argue publicly, never disclose case/client details in a public reply (confidentiality — this applies regardless of what the reviewer disclosed), always take the specific dispute to a private channel.

**Never do:**
- Write or commission a review on behalf of a client
- Offer any incentive for a review
- Ask only satisfied clients while silently skipping unhappy ones (gating)
- Dispute a real negative review by revealing case details

## Tracking
Log every request sent and outcome in `SEO_WEEKLY_OPERATIONS.md` under a "Reviews" section — this is a lightweight process, not a separate database, until volume justifies more.
