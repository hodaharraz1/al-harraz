# Google Review Growth Plan

Date: 2026-10-07. Zero-cost, manual, owner-executed plan. No code change
supports or automates any of this — reviews cannot be requested, posted,
or managed from this environment.

## Principles (non-negotiable)

- No incentivized reviews (no discounts, no gifts, no "leave a review for X").
- No staff-written reviews pretending to be clients.
- No mass review-request campaigns or review-gating (asking only happy
  clients while filtering out unhappy ones is against Google's policies).
- No review content is ever fabricated on the site itself — this is
  already a hard rule in `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md` and `LOCAL_SEO_PLAN.md`;
  restated here for the review-plan specifically.

## When to ask

- After a matter concludes with a resolved, positive outcome for the
  client — not mid-case, not immediately after a difficult or sensitive
  matter (family law, criminal defense) where asking could feel inappropriate
  or where the client may not want their association with the firm made
  more visible.
- Never ask a client to review a matter that is still confidential or
  ongoing.

## Simple WhatsApp request template (adapt tone as needed)

**AR:**
> شكرًا لثقتكم في مكتب آل حراز. لو حابب تساعدنا، تقدر تسيبلنا تقييم على جوجل من هنا: [GBP review link]. تقييمك بيفرق معانا ومع أي حد بيدور على محامي موثوق. وشكرًا تاني على وقتك.

**EN:**
> Thank you for trusting Al Harraz Law Firm with your matter. If you have a moment, a Google review would mean a lot to us: [GBP review link]. It genuinely helps others looking for a reliable lawyer find us. Thanks again for your time.

Send once. Do not follow up repeatedly if there's no response — that
crosses into pressure.

## Response workflow

**Positive reviews:**
- Thank the reviewer briefly and professionally. Do not mention any
  specific matter details, outcomes, or confidential information in the
  public reply — even details the reviewer themselves volunteered, since
  the firm's own reply is a separate public statement and should stay
  generic ("Thank you for your kind words and for trusting our team.").

**Negative reviews:**
- Respond calmly and professionally, without confirming or denying any
  client relationship or matter details publicly (confidentiality applies
  regardless of what the reviewer discloses).
- Never argue publicly or discuss case specifics.
- A short, professional acknowledgment and an invitation to discuss
  privately (phone/WhatsApp/email) is the safest pattern: "We take all
  feedback seriously. Please contact our office directly at [phone] so we
  can address this properly."
- If a negative review contains confidential matter details the client
  disclosed, do not confirm or elaborate on them in the reply — privilege
  and confidentiality obligations run to the firm, not just the client's
  choice to disclose publicly.

## What this plan explicitly avoids

- No review-gating or selective solicitation based on expected sentiment.
- No bulk SMS/WhatsApp blast to the full client list at once — one
  relevant case, one request, spaced naturally over time.
- No third-party review-management service that generates or incentivizes
  reviews.
